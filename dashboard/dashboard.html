Monthly sales trend

Total order value by month, Jan 2014 – Dec 2017. Strong Nov/Dec seasonality every year.

Monthly profit trend

Profit doesn't track sales 1:1 — several high-sales months (Jul '14, Jan '15) post a loss.

Headline finding: total sales are $2.30M with only $286K in profit — a 12.5% margin. The gap isn't random: it traces to three specific levers, one per tab above — a handful of loss-making product lines, discounts past 30%, and ten states that lose money despite real sales volume.

Profit margin % by sub-category

Sorted worst → best. Tables and Bookcases are net losses despite being top-5 in sales — the classic "growth trap."

Region × Category profit

Only one combination is outright unprofitable — but margins vary a lot beneath the surface.

States losing money

10 states post a net loss despite meaningful sales — Texas is the single worst market in the dataset.

Profit margin % by discount tier

The relationship is almost linear — every step up in discount trades margin away.

This is the single highest-leverage fix in the dataset: orders with no discount run a healthy 29.5% margin. Once discounts pass 30%, margin flips to -48.2% — those orders lose money on every dollar of sales. Capping standard discounts at 15% (12.8% margin, still positive) would protect the vast majority of at-risk revenue.

Profitability by customer segment

Home Office has the fewest customers but the best margin — Consumer has the most volume and the thinnest margin.

Avg. delivery days by ship mode
